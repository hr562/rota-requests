Privacy notice: Rota requests

This page explains how the Rosie Nightingale Homecare rota request form (https://hr562.github.io/rota-requests/) uses personal information. It is a working draft for the organisation to review.

Who is responsible

Rosie Nightingale Homecare, Bolton, United Kingdom. Contact: hr@rosienightingale.co.uk.

What the form collects

  • Your Google account email address and name, used only to check who you are when you sign in.
  • The details you type into a request: for example the type of change, dates and times, a service user's name, a reason, and notes.
  • The date and time of the request and what happens to it afterwards (decisions, who made them, when the rota was updated and checked).

Please do not enter addresses, phone numbers, health information or other sensitive details in the form.

Why it is collected

To record, approve, carry out and check changes to care visit rotas, and to keep a reliable record of who asked for what and when.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Requests are stored in the organisation's Google Workspace (a Google Sheet and Google Apps Script). Only authorised office staff can open the records. This web page is hosted on GitHub, but GitHub does not receive or store any request details. Carers can see only their own requests.

Sign-in

The form uses Google Sign-In. It reads only your email address and basic profile name. It does not read your Gmail, Drive, contacts or calendar.

How long it is kept

Rota records are kept for as long as the organisation's retention policy requires, then removed or archived.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or object to how it is used, by contacting hr@rosienightingale.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
